# GROUP REFLECTION

## Our Learning Experience

Throughout our laboratory activities, our group gained a better understanding of cloud computing and how it works in real-world IT environments. At the beginning, we were introduced to the basic concepts of cloud computing, including cloud service models, deployment models, and different cloud platforms. As we progressed through the activities, we became more familiar with how these concepts are applied through actual tools and configurations.

We realized that cloud computing involves more than simply storing files online. It also requires understanding how servers, networks, applications, databases, and security work together. Each laboratory activity introduced us to new concepts and gave us opportunities to apply what we learned through practical exercises.

## Skills and Knowledge We Gained

During the laboratory activities, we developed skills in using GitHub for documentation, executing Linux commands, managing Docker containers, and understanding different types of cloud storage. We learned the differences between block storage, file storage, and object storage, including their purposes and uses in different computing environments.

One of our most valuable experiences was our final laboratory project, where we deployed a private cloud storage system using Nextcloud and MariaDB through Docker Compose. We learned how to configure containers, connect application services to a database, and use persistent volumes to ensure that important data would not disappear when containers were restarted or removed. We also gained experience configuring the UFW firewall and creating a Bash script for automated database backups.

Through these activities, we understood that successfully running an application requires more than following installation instructions. It also requires proper configuration, testing, and attention to security and data protection.

## Challenges and Lessons Learned

During the activities, our group encountered several challenges, especially when configuring services and making different components work together. There were times when we had difficulty understanding configuration files, identifying errors, and connecting containers properly. Some tasks also required us to repeat commands and check our settings before achieving the expected results.

Although these challenges were sometimes frustrating, they helped us become more patient and responsible when solving technical problems. We learned not to rush through instructions and to carefully examine error messages, logs, and configurations. We also realized the importance of teamwork because sharing ideas and helping one another made difficult tasks easier to understand and complete.

## Understanding the Importance of Security and Maintenance

Our final project taught us that deploying a private cloud storage system is not the end of the process. A system must also be protected and maintained to ensure that it continues working properly. We learned the importance of firewall configuration, secure credentials, persistent storage, and regular database backups.

We also realized that protecting a complete Nextcloud deployment requires backing up not only the database but also uploaded files and important configuration data. This helped us understand why backup procedures should be properly planned and tested. In the future, we would like to improve our deployment by adding HTTPS, storing backups in another location, and regularly checking whether our recovery procedures work correctly.

## Application to Our Future Careers

The knowledge and experience we gained from these laboratory activities will be useful in our future studies and careers in information technology. Learning how to work with Linux, Docker, databases, networking, and cloud infrastructure has given us a stronger foundation for handling technical tasks and developing practical IT solutions.

Overall, these laboratory activities helped us understand that working in the IT field requires continuous learning, patience, problem-solving, and cooperation. Not every task was easy, but each challenge provided an opportunity to improve our skills and understanding. As a group, we are grateful for the experience because it allowed us to apply our classroom knowledge to actual laboratory projects and become more confident in working with cloud technologies.
